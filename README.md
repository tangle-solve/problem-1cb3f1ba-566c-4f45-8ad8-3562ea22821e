### CHEM-000227: All-weight extension of atomic ionization bounds
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Prove \(N_c(Z)<b(s)Z+c(s)Z^{1/3}\) for every \(s\ge1\), where \(b(s)=\max_{0\le t\le1}(1+t^{s-1})/(1+t^s)\) and \(c(s)\) is finite. The unresolved range is \(s>3\), including quantitative control of \(c(s)\).
- **Status:** Hundertmark, Pattakos and Schulz proved the weighted estimate for \(1<s\le3\) and explicitly conjectured its extension in 2025.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Since \(b(s)\to1\), the extension could substantially strengthen finite-atom ionization bounds.
- **Key works:** Hundertmark, Pattakos and Schulz (2025), *On the Excess Charge Problem of Atoms*, Remark 2.3, [arXiv:2504.18487](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.18487).
- **Related:** Analysis: weighted Hardy inequalities and uniform optimization bounds.
- **Source:** fleet:gpt6pro:chemistry:2026-10-06-gpt6pro-round2
